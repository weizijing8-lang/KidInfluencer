# Cross-Platform Analysis: YouTube vs TikTok - Key Findings

## TikTok Presence
- Family channels: 11/25 (44%) have active TikTok accounts (>1K followers)
- Adult channels: 12/13 (92%) have active TikTok accounts
- This is surprising — adult creators are MORE likely to be on TikTok

## But family channels that ARE on TikTok are massive:
- **Brent Rivera**: 50.6M TikTok followers, 1,765 videos
- **Piper Rockelle**: 20.1M followers, **3,889 videos** (more than YouTube!)
- **Rebecca Zamolo**: 18.5M followers, 3,765 videos
- **Pierson**: 17.3M followers, 1,604 videos

## Key Insight: TikTok as the PRIMARY platform for some family channels
- Piper Rockelle: 3,889 TikTok videos vs 816 YouTube videos (4.8x more on TikTok!)
- Rebecca Zamolo: 3,765 TikTok vs 1,171 YouTube (3.2x more)
- Brent Rivera: 1,765 TikTok vs 884 YouTube (2x more)
- These are all HIGH exploitation index channels

## This means our YouTube-only analysis UNDERESTIMATES labor exploitation:
- If a child is producing 3,889 TikTok videos + 816 YouTube videos = 4,705 total videos
- That's an even more extreme labor burden than YouTube alone suggests

## Exploitation Index vs TikTok:
- Correlation r=0.160, p=0.66 (not significant with small sample)
- But qualitatively: the top TikTok family channels (Brent, Piper, Rebecca, Pierson)
  are exactly the ones in the high-exploitation collaboration network we identified

## Engagement on TikTok:
- Family avg plays: 7.2M/video, Adult avg plays: 9.9M/video
- Family avg likes: 718K/video, Adult avg likes: 783K/video
- Adult channels get slightly more engagement per video on TikTok
- But family channels produce FAR more videos (especially Piper, Rebecca)
