## Revised Paper Narrative

The original "exploitation ratchet" hypothesis (viral hit → more exploitation) is NOT supported.
Instead, the data tells a different story:

1. **Exploitation is a STABLE TRAIT, not a dynamic process**: Channels that exploit children do so consistently from early on
2. **The algorithm REWARDS exploitation**: Within 18/23 family channels, more exploitative videos get more views
3. **But creators don't escalate**: The feedback loop doesn't lead to progressive escalation
4. **Comment ecology confirms exploitation**: High-exploitation videos attract more inappropriate viewers

This is actually a STRONGER finding for policy: exploitation is structural, not accidental.
Channels don't "drift" into exploitation — they are designed for it from the start.
