## Key Insights

### Different channels exploit in different ways:
- **Ryan's World**: Primarily LABOR exploitation (6.4 videos/week for 11 years) + massive network (24 partners)
- **Family Fizz**: Primarily COMMERCIAL exploitation (93% sponsored videos!)
- **Jordan Matter / Rebecca Zamolo**: Primarily NETWORK exploitation (hub of family channel collaborations)
- **Piper Rockelle**: Primarily CONTENT exploitation (high exploitation score in titles)

### The collaboration network reveals a "clique":
- Jordan Matter, Rebecca Zamolo, Piper Rockelle, Brent Rivera, Pierson Wodzynski form a tightly connected group
- These are all high-exploitation channels
- Suggests shared management/MCN driving exploitation patterns

### Labor exploitation is statistically significant:
- Family channels upload significantly more (Mann-Whitney p=0.037)
- Top family channels (Ryan's World, Bratayley) upload 5-6x per week = effectively full-time job for a child
- This is the most legally actionable finding (child labor law violations)

### Commercial exploitation is heterogeneous:
- Overall sponsorship rates are similar (family 12.3% vs adult 12.4%)
- But child brand targeting is 3.2x higher in family channels (20,073 vs 6,228 mentions)
- Some channels are almost entirely sponsored (Family Fizz 93%)
