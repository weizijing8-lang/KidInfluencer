## Key Insight for Paper 2

The collaboration network we detected from video data **maps directly onto real management structures**:

1. **Amp Studios** (Brent Rivera + Pierson) = our strongest collaboration edge (219 videos)
2. **Underscore Talent** (Rebecca Zamolo + Jordan Matter) = our second-strongest cluster

4. **Piper Rockelle** operates independently but connects to both clusters via collaborations

The "hub" nodes in our network (Jordan Matter, Rebecca Zamolo, Brent Rivera) are all managed by
professional talent agencies (Underscore, CAA, Amp Studios), confirming that the collaboration
patterns are **industry-driven, not organic**.
