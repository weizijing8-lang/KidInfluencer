# Annotation Guidelines: Kidfluencer Video Content Analysis

## Overview

You will annotate 100 YouTube videos on four dimensions. For each video, please watch at least the **first 30 seconds** and review the **title and thumbnail**. If the video is unavailable, mark all fields as "unavailable" in notes.

## Dimensions

### 1. Emotional Manipulation (`emotional_manipulation`)

Does the video use exaggerated emotions, staged conflicts, or emotional pressure on children to generate engagement?

| Label | Definition | Examples |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **none** | No emotional manipulation detected | Normal play, educational content, calm vlog |
| **mild** | Slightly exaggerated reactions, mild surprise/excitement | "OMG look at this!" with mild overreaction |
| **moderate** | Clearly staged emotional scenarios, children pressured to react | Pranks on children, fake "scary" situations, forced crying/screaming |
| **severe** | Extreme emotional distress, exploitation of children's emotions | Children visibly upset/crying for content, punishment videos, extreme fear |

### 2. Commercial Signals (`commercial_signals`)

Does the video contain advertising, sponsorship, or product promotion?

| Label | Definition | Examples |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **none** | No commercial content detected | Pure entertainment/vlog without products |
| **brand_mention** | Products/brands shown or mentioned but not explicitly sponsored | Unboxing without disclosure, brand-name toys visible |
| **sponsored** | Explicitly sponsored content (disclosed or clearly identifiable) | "#ad" in title/description, "thanks to [brand]" |
| **product_placement** | Integrated product promotion without clear disclosure | Extended focus on branded items, affiliate links |

### 3. Child Role (`child_role`)

What role does the child play in this video?

| Label | Definition | Examples |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **protagonist** | Child is the main subject/performer of the video | Child doing challenges, child's daily routine |
| **co_star** | Child appears alongside adults as a significant participant | Family activity where child and parent both featured |
| **cameo** | Child appears briefly or in the background | Parent's vlog where child walks through |
| **absent** | No child appears in the video | Adult-only content |
| **unclear** | Cannot determine from title/thumbnail/first 30 seconds | Ambiguous content |

### 4. Clickbait Level (`clickbait_level`)

How much does the title/thumbnail use clickbait tactics?

| Label | Definition | Examples |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **none** | Straightforward, descriptive title | "Morning routine with kids" |
| **mild** | Some exaggeration or curiosity gap | "You won't BELIEVE what happened!" |
| **moderate** | Significant exaggeration, misleading elements, ALL CAPS | "SHE CRIED!!! 😱 WORST DAY EVER" |
| **severe** | Extremely misleading, false claims, shock tactics | "CALLED 911!!!" (nothing happened), fake emergencies |

## Instructions

1. Open the YouTube URL for each video
2. Look at the title and thumbnail first
3. Watch at least the first 30 seconds of the video
4. Fill in your judgment for each dimension using the labels above
5. If you're unsure, use your best judgment and add a note in the "notes" column
6. If the video is unavailable/deleted, write "unavailable" in notes and leave other fields blank

## Important Notes

- Judge based on what you **observe**, not what you assume
- A video can have "severe" clickbait but "none" emotional manipulation (misleading title but harmless content)
- "Brand mention" includes unboxing videos where brands are shown even without explicit sponsorship
- For "child_role", judge based on who is the **focus** of the content, not just who appears

## Time Estimate

- ~1-2 minutes per video
- Total: ~2-3 hours for 100 videos

Thank you for your help!
