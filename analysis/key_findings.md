# Key Findings from Descriptive Analysis

## Dataset
- 38 valid channels (>= 1000 subs), 1,944 videos sampled
- 20 channels have 1M+ subs, 11 have 10M+ subs

## Key Findings

### 1. Emotional Titles Get ~2x More Views
- Emotional title videos: mean 3.0M views, median 0.1M
- Normal title videos: mean 1.3M views, median ~40K
- This supports the "platform incentive" hypothesis for Paper 1

### 2. High-Risk Channels Identified
- JesssFam: 34% emotional title rate
- Jordan Matter: 30% emotional title rate  
- Beast Family Vlogs: 26%
- Piper Rockelle: 22% (known controversy case)
- The Bucket List Family: 22%

### 3. Cross-Platform Ecosystem
- 68% have Instagram linked
- 18% have TikTok linked
- Average 1.4 other platforms linked

### 4. Commercial Detection Needs Work
- 0 commercial videos detected via keyword matching
- Need to improve detection (check video descriptions more thoroughly, use NLP)

### 5. Interesting Pattern: Smaller channels use more emotional titles
- The scatter plot shows emotional manipulation is concentrated in mid-tier channels (1-10M subs)
- Mega channels (100M+) have low emotional title rates (2-4%)
